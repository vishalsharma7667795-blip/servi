# Changelog

## 1.3.0 — SERVI logo redesign
- Replaced the letter-shaped orbital mark with a browser-style three-ribbon spiral in blue, cyan and violet.
- The app icon contains no letters; the word SERVI is shown separately in the app header and splash screen.
- Added a softly pulsing icon with a rotating luminous orbit during splash and in the browser header.
- Updated launcher icon asset to `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/servi_logo.png`.
- Updated Android version to 1.3.0 (versionCode 4).

## 1.2.0 — Advanced browser tools
- Added a lightweight tracker shield, privacy dashboard, reading view, find-in-page, Hindi translation shortcut, data saver and text zoom.
- Improved tab handling, renderer recovery, and privacy controls.
